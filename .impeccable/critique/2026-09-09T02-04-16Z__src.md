## Overall Impression

Light mode is correct, quiet, and slightly anemic. Structure, IA, and copy hold; the surfaces do not. Cards, tiles, and device shells sit too close to the page background, muted body/tag text risks contrast, and the two signature interactions (glare sweep, cursor glow) fade hardest exactly where a recruiter on a bright office screen will look. Dark mode sells craft; light mode whispers it.

Single biggest opportunity: restore separation and signal in light without adding shadow-soup — deepen the tonal step and make glow/glare adaptive so hover answers with the same confidence as dark.

## What's Working

1. **Token discipline.** `:root` light (`#F1F5F9` bg, white card, ink navy, mist line) vs `.dark` (`#0d1117`/`#131a22`) is cleanly split in `src/index.css`; kickers, numbers, tags stay cyan-only per One Voice Rule.
2. **Theme plumbing.** `theme-provider.tsx` (`light`/`dark`/`system` + `localStorage` + `prefers-color-scheme` listener) with `MutationObserver` in Skills/BorderGlow/SpecularButton keeps icons (`darkSrc` for Inertia/Railway) and blends (`soft-light`→`overlay`, `plus-lighter`→`normal`) coherent across toggle.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] What**: White glare on white card — `GlareHover glareColor #ffffff @0.3` over `var(--card)` white in light is invisible.
  **Why it matters**: Skill tiles are the proof-cloud; hover gives zero confirmation in light, flattening the tactile story PRODUCT.md sells as craft credential.
  **Fix**: Make glare adaptive — in light use cyan-tinted glare (`#00B8DB` at 0.12–0.18) or dark glare (`#0f172a` at 0.08), keep white only in dark. Gate on `isDark` already present in `Skills.tsx`.
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable polish

- **[P1] What**: Card/page separation collapse — white card on `#F1F5F9` + `oklch(0.925…)` mist hairline + no resting shadow; Safari shell `#E5E5E5` also sits inside white card.
  **Why it matters**: In daylight the featured/detail cards and device mocks bleed into the page; hierarchy must come from text alone, raising scan cost for rapid recruiter screening.
  **Fix**: In light only, deepen `--border` one step or add `border-border/80` emphasis on GlowCards + `ring-1 ring-slate-900/5`; tint Safari shell to `#DCE3EA` in light so chrome reads against white card. Keep dark untouched.
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable layout

- **[P1] What**: Muted + cyan-tag contrast risk in light — `muted-foreground oklch(0.56…)` body and `text-[#00B8DB]/80` 12px mono tags on `#F1F5F9`.
  **Why it matters**: Hero intro, nav names, skill proof suffixes, and project tags are the hiring scan path; if they dip under 4.5:1 on bright screens, speed-to-judgment (PRODUCT.md principle 2) fails.
  **Fix**: Darken light `muted-foreground` to `oklch(0.48–0.50 …)` or bump tags to full-opacity `#00A5C4` for text (keep `#00B8DB` for large graphics). Verify hero intro, nav, `0.65rem` kickers at 200% zoom.
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable audit

- **[P2] What**: Signature glow muted to haze — `BorderGlow` light multipliers `0.7`/`0.55` + `CursorGrid maxOpacity 0.6 radius 120` cyan on light slate.
  **Why it matters**: Hover aura that proves motion-craft in dark becomes a faint smudge in light; reduced perceived quality without any code error.
  **Fix**: Raise light glow to `0.75–0.85`, increase `CursorGrid` light `maxOpacity` to `0.8` or darken grid color to `#009BBD` in light only; keep fade timings (`0.25s` in / `0.75s` out).
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable polish

- **[P2] What**: SpecularButton rim light loses legibility in light — transparent bed, `#0f172a` text, `#94a3b8` base edge; WebGL rim tuned for dark.
  **Why it matters**: Primary CTA (Get in touch / Email me) is the conversion; if edge light blooms on dark but grays out on light, the action feels disabled to half the audience.
  **Fix**: In light, darken `resolvedBaseColor` to `#64748b`, keep `lineColor #00B8DB`, and confirm `:focus-visible` 2px outline passes on `#F1F5F9`. No copy change.
  **Suggested command**: /impeccable polish
